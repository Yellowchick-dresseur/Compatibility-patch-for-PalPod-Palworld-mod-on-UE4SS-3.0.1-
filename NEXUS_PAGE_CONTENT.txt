[size=5][b]PalPodFix - Compatibility Patch[/b][/size]

A community-made compatibility helper that makes [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/999]PalPod by Zurr[/url] work properly with the latest UE4SS (3.0.1+) on Steam.

[b][color=red]This is NOT a replacement for PalPod![/color][/b] You must install PalPod first. This patch only helps configure your local installation and adds a diagnostic companion mod. It contains no PalPod code or assets.

[size=4][b]What this patch does[/b][/size]

The included installer:

[list=1]
[*]Detects your Steam Palworld installation
[*]Detects your UE4SS version (3.0.1+ or legacy)
[*]Moves PalPod to the correct UE4SS folder structure
[*]Diagnoses PalPod's main.lua for known issues (does NOT modify it)
[*]Cleans duplicates in mods.txt
[*]Installs PalPodFix companion mod (F8 hotkey for diagnostics)
[*]Renames .mp3 to .wav in your Music folder (with your permission)
[/list]

Plus an [b]optional[/b] standalone helper script you can run yourself if you want to apply the main.lua fixes automatically on your local PalPod copy.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases]UE4SS 3.0.1+[/url]
[*]Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/999]PalPod[/url] (the actual music player)
[*]Run [b]Installer.bat[/b] from this archive
[*]Follow the prompts
[/list]

[size=4][b]In-game controls[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]N[/b] - Open/close PalPod menu (PalPod's binding)
[*][b]O[/b] - Toggle playback (PalPod's binding)
[*][b]F8[/b] - NEW: Show PalPodFix diagnostics in console
[/list]

[size=4][b]Adding your music[/b][/size]

PalPod only reads .wav files. Convert your MP3s to WAV with any tool, then drop them in:

[code]Pal\Content\Paks\LogicMods\PalPodData\Music\[/code]

In-game: open PalPod (N) -> Refresh -> your tracks appear.

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Random/Shuffle and Loop modes are buggy in PalPod's Blueprint and cannot be fixed by a Lua patch
[*]Game Pass version not supported (Steam only)
[/list]
